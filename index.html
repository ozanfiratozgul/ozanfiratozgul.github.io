About Me

I am a doctoral researcher at CARIM (Maastricht Uni.) where my research focuses on developing advanced biomedical signal processing and machine learning tools to personalize treatment of atrial fibrillation (AF) patients.

Before that, I was working as a data scientist in the Big Data Group of STM (Ankara).

Lately, my interest in blog keeping has increased, and this is the place I plan to materialize my thoughts!
