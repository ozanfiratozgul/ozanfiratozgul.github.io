I am a researcher specializing in cardiovascular data science at CARIM, where I focus on developing advanced biomedical signal processing and machine learning tools to personalize treatments for patients with atrial fibrillation (AF). Previously, I worked as a data scientist in the Big Data Group at STM (Ankara). Recently, I have cultivated a passion for sharing knowledge and exploring ideas through blogging, and this platform serves as a space to bring those thoughts to life.
